# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Define the decision and user** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Set thresholds and abstention zones** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Design meaningful human oversight** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Embed the output in a signal-to-action pathway** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Measure human–AI and managerial performance** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Feed results into learning and governance** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

# Chapter 12: Decision Architecture and Lifecycle Governance

**Part IV — Governance, Evidence, and Lifecycle Decision-Making**

This folder contains the canonical resources aligned principally with Chapter 12. The chapter establishes the governing conceptual and methodological context; the companion resources support structured application, documentation, analysis, teaching, and local adaptation.

Resources should be selected according to the decision being addressed. No favourable score or computational result overrides a mandatory legal, clinical, ethical, privacy, cybersecurity, quality, equity, procurement, or institutional safeguard.

## Core institutional records

- [AIHE-D02 — Lifecycle Decision State, Staged Evidence, and AI-HED Status](core/AIHE-D02) — Record the current institutional decision state, exact intervention identity, scope, evidence, conditions, safeguards, triggers, authority, history, and next review.
- [AIHE-D03 — Post-Deployment Monitoring and Algorithmovigilance Cadence](core/AIHE-D03) — Define recurring and event-triggered surveillance across technical, workflow, safety, equity, economic, and contractual domains.
- [AIHE-D04 — Incident Classification and Reconstruction](core/AIHE-D04) — Reconstruct technical, clinical, workflow, privacy, cybersecurity, equity, communication, and economic incidents consistently.
- [AIHE-D05 — Material Change, Reauthorization, Renewal, Replacement, and Retirement Record](core/AIHE-D05) — Assess material change, revalidation, current authorization, renewal, renegotiation, replacement, retirement, continuity, and the end of authorization.

## Python analytical modules

- [AIHE-PY09 — Temporal validation and drift monitoring](python_analytics/PY09_drift) — Tracks discrimination, calibration error, score distribution, and feature drift across time periods.
- [AIHE-PY27 — Algorithmovigilance, lifecycle cadence, drift, and incident monitoring](python_analytics/PY27_monitoring) — Monitors technical, clinical, workflow, equity, economic, and trust indicators against warning and stop thresholds over time.
- [AIHE-PY29 — AI-HED Decision-Provenance and Lifecycle-Trigger Audit](python_analytics/PY29_decision_audit) — Audit a versioned AI-HED for missing authoritative records, conflicting intervention identities, overdue review, unresolved lifecycle triggers, and absent accountable authority without producing a composite approval score.
- [AIHE-PY40 — Lifecycle Decision-State and Trigger Review](python_analytics/PY40_decision_state) — Provide an advisory review of the current institutional decision state, conditions, safeguards, transition triggers, authority, and next review without issuing an institutional authorization.

# Chapter 6: Lifecycle Cost, Pricing, and Benefit Realization

**Part III — Economic Evaluation, Decision Modelling, and Procurement**

This folder contains the canonical resources aligned principally with Chapter 6. The chapter establishes the governing conceptual and methodological context; the companion resources support structured application, documentation, analysis, teaching, and local adaptation.

Resources should be selected according to the decision being addressed. No favourable score or computational result overrides a mandatory legal, clinical, ethical, privacy, cybersecurity, quality, equity, procurement, or institutional safeguard.

## Core institutional records

- [AIHE-B01 — Economic Evaluation Specification](core/AIHE-B01) — Define the economic decision, perspective, comparator, horizon, outcomes, costs, methods, uncertainty, safeguards, and validation.
- [AIHE-B02 — Lifecycle Cost and Pricing Record](core/AIHE-B02) — Record acquisition through exit costs, resource use, pricing exposure, opportunity cost, residual value, and evidence.

## Worked examples

- [AIHE-CASE06 — Five-Year Lifecycle Cost, Pricing, ROI, and Benefit Realization](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE06_five_year_lifecycle_cost_pricing_roi_and_benefit_realization) — Does the proposed AI service create value after all lifecycle costs, pricing exposure, and benefit-conversion conditions are included?
- [AIHE-CASE06B — Financial Recognition and Budget Incidence](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE06B_financial_recognition_and_budget_incidence) — How should released capacity, avoided resource use, cashable savings, revenue, accounting treatment, and budget incidence be distinguished?

## Python analytical modules

- [AIHE-PY10 — Total Cost of AI Ownership (TCAIO) lifecycle costing](python_analytics/PY10_cost_budget) — Calculates one-time and recurring AI costs, discounted present value, cost per use, and optimistic/base/conservative scenarios across the full lifecycle.
- [AIHE-PY11 — ROI, net benefit, and benefit-realization analysis](python_analytics/PY11_finance_value) — Calculates gross and risk-adjusted benefits, total cost, net benefit, ROI, payback period, and the effect of realization probability and attribution assumptions.

## Supplemental specialist resources

# AIHE-RG04 — Regulatory, Standards, and Policy Update Register

**Primary chapter:** 10 — Governance, Accountability, and Institutional Risk 
**Additional chapter alignment:** 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12-15 
**Version:** V1.0

## Purpose

Maintain date-stamped records for European, Romanian, and global legal, policy, standards, reimbursement, and guidance sources whose scope or implementation status may change.

Use the narrative document for interpretation and the spreadsheet for a controlled, updateable record. Verify the current status and edition of external instruments before institutional use.

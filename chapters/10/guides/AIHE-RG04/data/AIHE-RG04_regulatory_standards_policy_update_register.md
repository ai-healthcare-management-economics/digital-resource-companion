# AIHE-RG04 — Regulatory, Standards, and Policy Update Register

**Aligned chapters:** 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12 
**Primary use:** preserve a date-stamped record of external instruments that may change between book editions or institutional reviews.

## Instructions

1. Record the authoritative source rather than a secondary summary.
2. Distinguish adopted law from proposal, consultation, guidance, standard, national strategy, or institutional policy.
3. Record the exact consolidated version, amendment, correction, implementation phase, and date accessed.
4. Identify the decision domains affected: evidence, data, procurement, quality, governance, reimbursement, sustainability, or lifecycle control.
5. Assign an owner and next review date.
6. Do not allow an outdated date in a textbook or template to control a current institutional decision.

---

These guides supplement the stable scholarly text. They are not legal, regulatory, clinical, accreditation, procurement, cybersecurity, or HTA advice. Verify current law, standards, official guidance, reimbursement rules, and institutional policy immediately before use. Preserve the resource ID, version, evidence cut-off, local adaptations, accountable owner, and next review date.

# AIHE-EX08: Vendor Demonstration Evaluation Form

Primary lifecycle stage: Procure and contract. Primary book chapter: 9.

## Process steps

### 1. Define problem-first requirements
Translate the service need, evidence, workflow, safeguards, and lifecycle expectations into Vendor Demonstration Evaluation Form.

### 2. Challenge vendor claims and compare options
Assess exact version, evidence, local applicability, interoperability, price, support, and risk.

### 3. Conduct demonstrations and due diligence
Test representative workflows, limitations, integration, monitoring, security, and vendor capability.

### 4. Negotiate lifecycle contract terms
Specify audit, updates, data rights, service levels, pricing, remedies, portability, take-back, and exit.

### 5. Approve conditionally and retain leverage
Use gates, pilot criteria, performance review, renewal thresholds, and termination readiness.

## Illustrative application

Structure demonstrations around real workflows, evidence, limitations, usability, interoperability, monitoring, and unsupported claims.

This process flow is a navigational and decision-support aid. It is not a validated clinical, regulatory, legal, procurement, cybersecurity, accreditation, or HTA instrument. Adapt it to the relevant jurisdiction, institution, population, workflow, technology version, evidence cut-off, and accountable authority.

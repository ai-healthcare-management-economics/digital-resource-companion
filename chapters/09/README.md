# Chapter 9: Problem-First Procurement and Vendor Governance

**Part III — Economic Evaluation, Decision Modelling, and Procurement**

This folder contains the canonical resources aligned principally with Chapter 9. The chapter establishes the governing conceptual and methodological context; the companion resources support structured application, documentation, analysis, teaching, and local adaptation.

Resources should be selected according to the decision being addressed. No favourable score or computational result overrides a mandatory legal, clinical, ethical, privacy, cybersecurity, quality, equity, procurement, or institutional safeguard.

## Core institutional records

- [AIHE-D01 — Vendor Evidence and Contract Requirements](core/AIHE-D01) — Translate evidence, interoperability, data, security, monitoring, update, service, remedy, portability, and exit requirements into contract terms.

## Worked examples

- [AIHE-CASE09 — AI Vendor Procurement and Contract Negotiation](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE09_ai_vendor_procurement_and_contract_negotiation) — How should the hospital compare vendors when a lower price is accompanied by weak audit, security, update, and exit provisions?
- [AIHE-CASE09B — Upstream Model-Provider Change During a Vendor Contract](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE09B_upstream_model_provider_change_during_vendor_contract) — Does a change in the underlying model or hosting dependency require disclosure, revalidation, contractual action, and reauthorization?

## Python analytical modules

- [AIHE-PY17 — AI pricing and contract scn analysis](python_analytics/PY17_pricing_contract) — Compares subscription, per-use, risk-sharing, and hybrid pricing arrangements under different volume, uptake, performance, and savings assumptions.

# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Translate service needs into lifecycle procurement criteria** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Assess the current asset and dependencies** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Compare maintain, repair, refurbish, replace, and share options** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Validate safety, regulatory, quality, and continuity conditions** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Contract for support, residual value, and end of life** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Implement the selected option** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Decommission and close responsibly** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

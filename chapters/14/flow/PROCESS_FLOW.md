# Chapter 14: Circular Procurement and Asset Stewardship

Chapter-level decision process aligned to the fifteen-chapter reference edition.

## Process steps

### 1. Translate service needs into lifecycle procurement criteria
Specify durability, modularity, repairability, interoperability, support, take-back, and data requirements.

### 2. Assess the current asset and dependencies
Review condition, remaining life, software support, cybersecurity, validation, workload, downtime, and supply chain.

### 3. Compare maintain, repair, refurbish, replace, and share options
Use the same service requirement, horizon, safety constraints, and cost boundary.

### 4. Validate safety, regulatory, quality, and continuity conditions
Confirm infection prevention, device requirements, data erasure, recipient due diligence, and fallback.

### 5. Contract for support, residual value, and end of life
Define components, updates, take-back, recovery evidence, pricing, remedies, and exit.

### 6. Implement the selected option
Plan transition, training, downtime, testing, communication, and monitoring.

### 7. Decommission and close responsibly
Preserve records, erase data, maintain chain of custody, verify disposition, and recognize residual or recovery value.

## Illustrative application

An imaging platform is compared across maintenance, refurbishment, replacement, and shared-service options with secure decommissioning conditions.

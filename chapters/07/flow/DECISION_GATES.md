# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Specify the economic and HTA decision** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Select the evaluation form** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Estimate incremental costs and outcomes** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Assess affordability and reimbursement** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Characterize uncertainty and distribution** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Integrate HTA, procurement, and local readiness** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Issue a conditional recommendation** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

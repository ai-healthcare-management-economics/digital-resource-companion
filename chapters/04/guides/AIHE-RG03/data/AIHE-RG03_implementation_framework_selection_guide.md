# AIHE-RG03 — Implementation Framework Selection Guide

**Aligned chapters:** 4, 11, and 12 
**Primary use:** select implementation theories, models, or frameworks according to the question being asked.

## Functional selection

| Question | Useful framework or theory | Status and contribution | Limitation in healthcare AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which multilevel determinants may influence implementation? | Updated CFIR | Established determinant framework; organizes innovation, outer setting, inner setting, individuals, and implementation process | Does not itself specify economic evaluation, algorithmic performance, or regulatory conformity |
| Why might technology-supported change be rejected, abandoned, fail to scale, or fail to sustain? | NASSS and NASSS-CAT | Established complexity-oriented framework and assessment tool for technology-supported change | Requires separate clinical, technical, economic, equity, and safety evidence |
| Who is reached, what changes, who adopts, how faithfully is it implemented, and is it maintained? | RE-AIM | Established implementation-outcome framework | Outcome dimensions require locally valid measures and causal interpretation |
| How do context, implementation, outcomes, adaptation, and sustainability interact? | PRISM with RE-AIM | Established implementation and sustainability model that integrates context and outcome dimensions | Not an AI-specific governance or assurance standard |
| Will users accept or intend to use the technology? | TAM and UTAUT | Established acceptance theories focused on usefulness, effort, social influence, facilitating conditions, and intention | Acceptance is neither safe use nor sustained clinical/economic value |
| How should complex-intervention development and evaluation proceed? | MRC complex-intervention guidance | Established research guidance for programme theory, context, evaluation, implementation, and economic considerations | Requires adaptation to AI versioning, data drift, and lifecycle controls |
| How should qualitative and quantitative implementation evidence be integrated? | Mixed-method designs and JBI convergent synthesis approaches | Established methodological approaches for integrating different evidence types | Integration method must be prespecified; incompatible constructs should not be combined mechanically |
| How should confidence in qualitative synthesis findings be judged? | GRADE-CERQual | Established confidence-appraisal approach | Applies to qualitative synthesis findings, not individual interviews or quantitative outcomes |

## Decision rules

- Use no more frameworks than the decision requires.
- State whether each framework is used prospectively for planning, during implementation for adaptation, or retrospectively for explanation.
- Define constructs operationally and link each to data, an owner, timing, and a decision.
- Do not label a project “CFIR-based” or “RE-AIM-based” when only the headings are reproduced without theory-informed measurement or analysis.
- Combine acceptance theory with organizational, workflow, safety, economic, and lifecycle analysis for consequential AI.

# Chapter 13: Circular Health Systems and Resource Stewardship

**Part V — Circular Economy and Sustainable Value**

This folder contains the canonical resources aligned principally with Chapter 13. The chapter establishes the governing conceptual and methodological context; the companion resources support structured application, documentation, analysis, teaching, and local adaptation.

Resources should be selected according to the decision being addressed. No favourable score or computational result overrides a mandatory legal, clinical, ethical, privacy, cybersecurity, quality, equity, procurement, or institutional safeguard.

## Core institutional records

- [AIHE-E01 — Circular-Economy and Sustainable-AI Assessment](core/AIHE-E01) — Apply the circularity hierarchy from prevention and reduction through sharing, maintenance, reuse, refurbishment, recovery, and disposal.

## Worked examples

- [AIHE-CASE13 — Circular AI Infrastructure Strategy](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE13_circular_ai_infrastructure_strategy) — Can the health system meet its analytic need through avoidance, optimization, sharing, and life extension before acquiring new infrastructure?
- [AIHE-CASE13B — Rebound and Burden Transfer](../../worked_examples/AIHE-CASE13B_rebound_and_burden_transfer) — Can lower resource intensity coexist with higher total demand or burdens shifted across places, suppliers, populations, or periods?

## Python analytical modules

- [AIHE-PY36 — Circular Stock-Flow, Value-Retention, and Leakage Accounting](python_analytics/PY36_circular_flow) — Quantify resource stocks, flows, inner and outer value-retention loops, leakage, intensity, and absolute demand for AI-enabled services.
- [AIHE-PY39 — Rebound, Induced Demand, and Burden-Transfer Analysis](python_analytics/PY39_rebound_burden_transfer) — Assess whether lower resource intensity reduces absolute demand and whether costs, environmental effects, material burdens, or labour consequences are shifted across places, populations, suppliers, or periods.

## Supplemental specialist resources

Additional specialist resources are stored within the relevant core-resource folders. They preserve stable resource identifiers and provide more focused records, exercises, reference material, or structured data where a decision requires greater detail.

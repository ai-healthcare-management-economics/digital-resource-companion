# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Define the service need and functional output** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Prevent or avoid unnecessary demand** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Reduce and optimize resource intensity** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Share and intensify safe use** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Maintain, repair, reuse, and refurbish** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Recover materials and dispose responsibly** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Monitor rebound, burden shifting, and equity** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

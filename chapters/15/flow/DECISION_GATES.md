# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Define the decision, functional unit, and system boundary** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Estimate conventional outcomes and lifecycle cost** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Measure environmental and material flows** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Estimate residual, recovered, and resilience value** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Assess equity and burden distribution** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Monetize cautiously or preserve disaggregated evidence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Test uncertainty, rebound, and robust decisions** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 8: Issue a transparent decision with safeguards** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

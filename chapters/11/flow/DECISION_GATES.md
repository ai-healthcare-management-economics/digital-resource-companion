# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Formulate the evidence question** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Map and appraise external evidence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Plan local validation and silent-mode evaluation** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Select a causal evaluation design** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Collect real-world clinical and implementation evidence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Synthesize quantitative and qualitative evidence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Update the decision and evidence plan** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

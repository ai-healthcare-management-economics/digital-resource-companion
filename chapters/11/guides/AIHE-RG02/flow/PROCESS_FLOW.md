# AIHE-RG02 — Reporting and Appraisal Tool Selection Guide

## 1. Define the question

State the selection, verification, comparison, or update decision.

## 2. Identify applicable options

List relevant instruments, scenarios, evidence domains, or failure patterns.

## 3. Verify status and scope

Record authority, edition, jurisdiction, purpose, and limits.

## 4. Complete the local record

Document evidence, judgement, ownership, gaps, and action.

## 5. Review and update

Approve, archive, and set the next review or trigger.

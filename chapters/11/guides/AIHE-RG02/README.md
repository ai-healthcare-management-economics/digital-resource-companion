# AIHE-RG02 — Reporting and Appraisal Tool Selection Guide

**Primary chapter:** 11 — Evidence, Validation, and Real-World Evaluation 
**Additional chapter alignment:** 7, 8, 11 
**Version:** V1.0

## Purpose

Match evidence type and study stage to current reporting and appraisal instruments without treating checklist completion as proof of validity, effectiveness, or regulatory conformity.

Use the narrative document for interpretation and the spreadsheet for a controlled, updateable record. Verify the current status and edition of external instruments before institutional use.

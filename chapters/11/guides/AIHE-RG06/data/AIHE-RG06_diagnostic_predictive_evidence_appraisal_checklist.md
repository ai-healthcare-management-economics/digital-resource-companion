# AIHE-RG06 — Diagnostic and Predictive Evidence Appraisal Checklist

**Aligned chapters:** 3, 7, 8, and 11 
**Primary use:** connect statistical performance with intended use, bias, clinical utility, workflow, and resource consequences.

## Appraisal domains

| Domain | Questions |
|---|---|
| Intended use and pathway | What decision, user, population, setting, timing, and action does the system support? Is the comparator realistic? |
| Dataset construction | How were participants, encounters, sites, and time periods selected? Are exclusions and missingness explained? Is there leakage between development and evaluation data? |
| Index test or model | Is the exact model/version described? Were thresholds prespecified? Were prompts, templates, preprocessing, and human review defined where relevant? |
| Reference standard | Is the reference standard appropriate, independent, consistently applied, and blinded where feasible? Could incorporation or verification bias occur? |
| Discrimination and accuracy | Are sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, likelihood ratios, AUROC or other task-appropriate measures reported with uncertainty? |
| Calibration and thresholds | Are calibration-in-the-large, slope, plots, and threshold-specific consequences reported? Is recalibration needed locally? |
| Spectrum and transportability | Does the evaluation represent the intended population, prevalence, workflow, equipment, staffing, and service context? |
| Subgroups and equity | Are relevant groups sufficiently represented? Are error, calibration, access, and downstream consequences assessed by subgroup? |
| Human–AI performance | How do users interpret, accept, override, defer, or act on outputs? Is review burden and automation bias measured? |
| Clinical utility | Does the system improve decisions or outcomes compared with realistic alternatives? Is decision-curve or other consequence-sensitive analysis appropriate? |
| Economic consequences | What follow-up tests, referrals, staff time, delays, capacity demands, avoided events, and budget effects follow from true and false results? |
| Lifecycle evidence | How will drift, data changes, software/model updates, incidents, and local performance be monitored? |
| Reporting and appraisal | Which current reporting guideline and appraisal instrument apply, and which questions remain outside them? |

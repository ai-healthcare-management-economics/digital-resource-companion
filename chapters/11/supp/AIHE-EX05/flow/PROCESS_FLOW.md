# AIHE-EX05 — Local Validation Report Template

V1.0 · Process support aligned with the revised book.

## 1. Define the local decision and intended use

Specify the population, setting, user, decision window, comparator, expected action, consequence of error, accountable owner, and exact system version.

**Required input:** Approved use-case specification and exact system/version

**Expected output:** Local validation scope and intended-use statement

**Decision:** Scope is sufficiently precise for local validation

## 2. Define the validation cohort and evidence period

Record inclusion and exclusion criteria, data provenance, reference standard, missingness, representation, timing, and transformations or mappings.

**Required input:** Local cohort data, provenance, reference standard, and data-quality evidence

**Expected output:** Versioned validation cohort and data-fitness record

**Decision:** Cohort and data are fit for the stated validation question

## 3. Evaluate predictive or diagnostic performance

Assess discrimination, calibration, sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, error burden, threshold-specific performance, and uncertainty as appropriate.

**Required input:** Predictions, outcomes/reference standard, thresholds, and analysis plan

**Expected output:** Threshold-specific local performance results with uncertainty

**Decision:** Performance is interpretable against predefined requirements

## 4. Examine subgroup and workflow performance

Assess relevant subgroups, human–AI interaction, response capacity, overrides, action latency, workarounds, and balancing measures.

**Required input:** Subgroup variables, workflow observations, response data, and balancing measures

**Expected output:** Subgroup and workflow assessment

**Decision:** No unresolved subgroup or workflow issue prevents the next step

## 5. Assess clinical utility and decision consequences

Determine whether the output improves a defined decision or pathway relative to the comparator; keep validation separate from causal claims about downstream outcomes.

**Required input:** Comparator pathway, decision thresholds, downstream actions, and utility evidence

**Expected output:** Clinical-utility and decision-consequence assessment

**Decision:** The system has a defensible use case or requires further evaluation

## 6. Record limitations and decision conditions

State evidence gaps, mitigation, monitoring, restrictions, additional evidence, and conditions for proceeding, piloting, recalibrating, deferring, or stopping.

**Required input:** All local validation findings and unresolved uncertainties

**Expected output:** Conditional recommendation with safeguards and evidence needs

**Decision:** Decision conditions and non-compensatory safeguards are explicit

## 7. Approve and preserve the versioned record

Record accountable authority, evidence cut-off, decision date, dissent, next review date, and triggers for revalidation after material change.

**Required input:** Completed validation report and conditional recommendation

**Expected output:** Approved, archived, versioned local-validation record

**Decision:** Proceed, pilot, recalibrate, defer, restrict, or stop

A process step is not an institutional permission state. Preserve current authority, conditions, review triggers, and the ability to return to earlier assessment.

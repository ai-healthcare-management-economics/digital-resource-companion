# AIHE-EX05 — Local Validation Report Template: Process Flow

## Primary purpose

Document local evidence for the exact system, version, population, workflow, threshold, and decision being considered. Local validation should determine whether evidence generated elsewhere is sufficiently transportable and whether the system can be used safely and usefully under local conditions.

## Process steps

### 1. Define the local decision and intended use
Specify the population, setting, user, decision window, comparator, expected action, consequence of error, accountable owner, and exact system version.

### 2. Define the validation cohort and evidence period
Record inclusion and exclusion criteria, data provenance, reference standard, missingness, representation, timing, and any transformations or mappings.

### 3. Evaluate predictive or diagnostic performance
Assess discrimination, calibration, sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, error burden, and threshold-specific performance as appropriate to the use case. Report uncertainty rather than relying on point estimates alone.

### 4. Examine subgroup and workflow performance
Assess clinically and socially relevant subgroups, human–AI interaction, response capacity, overrides, action latency, workarounds, and balancing measures.

### 5. Assess clinical utility and decision consequences
Determine whether the output improves a defined decision or pathway relative to the comparator. Separate model validation from causal claims about downstream service outcomes.

### 6. Record limitations and decision conditions
State unresolved evidence gaps, mitigation, monitoring requirements, restrictions, additional evidence, and the conditions for proceeding, piloting, recalibrating, deferring, or stopping.

### 7. Approve and preserve the versioned record
Record the accountable decision-maker, evidence cut-off, decision date, dissent, next review date, and triggers for revalidation after material change.

## Status and limitations

This flow is an institutional decision-support aid. It is not a validated clinical, regulatory, legal, procurement, cybersecurity, accreditation, or HTA instrument. Adapt it to the relevant jurisdiction, institution, population, workflow, technology version, evidence cut-off, and accountable authority.

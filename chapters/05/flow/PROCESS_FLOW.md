# Chapter 5: Quality Management, Accreditation, and Organizational Learning

Chapter-level decision process aligned to the fifteen-chapter reference edition.

## Process steps

### 1. Translate requirements into measurable controls
Connect quality, safety, accreditation, and governance requirements to indicators and evidence.

### 2. Establish baselines and control limits
Define data sources, versions, acceptable ranges, review frequency, owners, and balancing indicators.

### 3. Monitor process, outcome, and experience
Review technical, human–AI, clinical, workflow, equity, economic, and patient-reported signals.

### 4. Detect nonconformity or adverse trends
Trigger review for control-limit breaches, incidents, complaints, audit findings, or accreditation gaps.

### 5. Investigate and apply corrective action
Reconstruct the event, identify causes, contain risk, and implement corrective and preventive action.

### 6. Verify effectiveness and close
Confirm remediation, residual risk, evidence quality, authority, and next review.

### 7. Convert findings into organizational learning
Update standards, training, design, procurement, and portfolio governance.

## Illustrative application

A diagnostic AI system shows subgroup drift and triggers a quality-control investigation, corrective action, and accreditation evidence update.

## Status and limitations

This process flow is a navigational and decision-support aid. It is not a validated clinical, regulatory, legal, procurement, cybersecurity, accreditation, or HTA instrument. Adapt it to the relevant jurisdiction, institution, population, workflow, technology version, evidence cut-off, and accountable authority.

# AIHE-RG07 — Common AI Adoption Failures and Corrective Actions

**Primary chapter:** 1 — AI as a Health System Intervention 
**Additional chapter alignment:** 1-12 
**Version:** V1.0

## Purpose

Identify recurring technology-led, economic, workflow, evidence, governance, procurement, and lifecycle failures and record preventive controls.

Use the narrative document for interpretation and the spreadsheet for a controlled, updateable record. Verify the current status and edition of external instruments before institutional use.

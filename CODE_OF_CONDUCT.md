# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository supports scholarly, professional, and educational use of the Digital Resource Companion. Participation should promote respectful collaboration, scientific integrity, privacy, patient safety, accessibility, equity, and reproducibility.

## Expected conduct

Contributors and users should:

- communicate respectfully and evaluate ideas rather than individuals;
- distinguish evidence, judgement, assumptions, and advocacy;
- avoid sharing identifiable patient information, credentials, private keys, confidential contracts, or restricted institutional material;
- respect intellectual-property rights, licences, attribution requirements, and publisher agreements;
- report limitations, conflicts of interest, and material uncertainty transparently;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, threatening, or deceptive conduct;
- support accessible formats and inclusive participation;
- preserve stable identifiers and document material changes; and
- avoid representing an author-developed framework, browser form, synthetic example, or computational result as a validated clinical or regulatory instrument.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or refer serious matters to the relevant institutional authority. A private maintainer contact channel should be designated before public release.

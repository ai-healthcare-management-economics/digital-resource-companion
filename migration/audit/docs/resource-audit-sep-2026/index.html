Book–Resource Revision Audit

Artificial Intelligence and Healthcare Economics — alignment of the Digital Resource Companion with the revised September 2026 book.

Executive determination

The companion needs a substantive revision, not merely replacement image files. The five-Part, fifteen-chapter architecture remains, but the revised edition changes the conceptual hierarchy, reduces overlapping figures and tables, strengthens decision provenance and lifecycle governance, and replaces the principal Chapter 15 case.

Worked examples

All current chapter cases remain useful except the Chapter 15 principal case, which should be replaced by an AI-assisted remote diagnostic pathway. Four concise additions are recommended: financial recognition and budget incidence; EVSI/ENBS for staged pilot design; upstream model-provider change during a contract; and rebound/burden transfer.

Computational resources

Priority revisions concern PY10/PY11, PY14/PY15, PY29, PY36/PY39, PY37 and PY38. An optional PY40 can implement a transparent decision-state and trigger engine whose output remains subject to accountable human authorization.

Website and bundles

The website should be regenerated from revised catalogs. The combined forms and workbooks should be regenerated from revised source records rather than edited independently. Complete machine-readable instructions are supplied in the accompanying CSV registers.

Access status

The book PDFs were available for full structural comparison. The attached ZIP archives were not available as extractable filesystem objects in this run, so this page and the accompanying files constitute an integration specification rather than a final replacement release.

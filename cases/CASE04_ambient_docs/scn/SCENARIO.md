## Decision question

How much of the claimed notes time saving becomes usable capacity after review, correction, adoption, and implementation costs?

## Scenario

A fictional university hospital considers a cloud-based ambient notes assistant for forty outpatient clinicians. The vendor reports seven minutes saved per encounter. Local review and correction are expected to absorb half of that saving, and no headcount reduction is assumed.

## Objectives

- Decompose gross productivity claims into net and realized value.
- Separate capacity release from cashable savings.
- Measure correction burden and quality alongside time.
- Design a controlled pilot with role and training requirements.

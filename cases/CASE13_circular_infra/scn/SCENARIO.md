## Decision question

Can the health system meet its analytic need through avoidance, optimization, sharing, and life extension before acquiring new infrastructure?

## Scenario

A fictional health system expects growing AI compute demand and is considering a new on-premises GPU cluster. Alternatives include model optimization, shared regional capacity, and cloud services. The decision compares cost, utilization, energy, water, hardware, resilience, data governance, and rebound risk.

## Objectives

- Apply the circularity hierarchy before new acquisition.
- Normalize resource use by useful output.
- Assess rebound and burden shifting.
- Integrate resilience and governance with environmental evidence.

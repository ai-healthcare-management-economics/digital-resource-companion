## Decision question

Which uncertainty is most likely to change the adoption decision, and is further evidence worth its cost?

## Scenario

A fictional radiology service considers an AI worklist-prioritization system for 10,000 studies per year. Benefits depend on prevalence, sensitivity, specificity, earlier treatment, and false-positive follow-up cost. The committee must identify the parameters that drive net benefit and decide whether further evidence is worthwhile.

## Objectives

- Specify a transparent decision model.
- Connect diagnostic performance with pathway consequences.
- Conduct threshold and scn analysis.
- Use value-of-information logic to prioritize evidence.

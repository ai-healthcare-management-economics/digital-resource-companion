# Worked solution: Remote Monitoring: Cost-Effectiveness, Budget Impact, and HTA

## Interpretation

The incremental cost is 250 per patient and the modeled gain is 0.03 QALYs, producing an ICER of about 8,333 per QALY and positive net monetary benefit at a threshold of 50,000. The budget-impact result is different: implementation and uptake create a positive cash requirement. The recommended decision is conditional adoption with staged uptake, funded response capacity, reimbursement clarification, and monitoring of digital exclusion.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

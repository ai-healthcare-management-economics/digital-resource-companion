# Worked solution: AI Vendor Procurement and Contract Negotiation

## Interpretation

Vendor A fails several mandatory safeguards, so its weighted average cannot justify selection. Vendor B may proceed to a controlled pilot after local evidence is verified, variable costs are capped, and monitoring and support are translated into enforceable service levels. Procurement value is the expected future value of the complete contract, not the lowest purchase price.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

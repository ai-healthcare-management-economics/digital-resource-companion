# Worked solution: AI Quality-Control, Accreditation, and Corrective Action

## Interpretation

The month-eight change breaches the false-negative limit and coincides with higher alert volume, downtime, and complaints. Immediate containment should restrict the updated version while the team reconstructs data, model, interface, and workflow changes. Closure requires evidence that corrective action restored performance, affected records and users were addressed, and version and update controls were improved.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

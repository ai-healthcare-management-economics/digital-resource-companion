# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Translate requirements into measurable controls** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Establish baselines and control limits** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Monitor process, outcome, and experience** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Detect nonconformity or adverse trends** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Investigate and apply corrective action** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Verify effectiveness and close** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Convert findings into organizational learning** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

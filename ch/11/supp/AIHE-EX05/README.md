# AIHE-EX05 — Local Validation Report Template

**Primary chapter:** 11 — Evidence, Validation, and Real-World Evaluation 
**Aligned chapters:** 3, 8, 11, and 12 
**Related resources:** AIHE-B05, AIHE-RG02, AIHE-RG06, AIHE-CASE11, AIHE-V 
**Version:** V1.0

## Purpose

Use this resource to document whether the exact system and version being considered for institutional use has been evaluated adequately in the intended local population, workflow, and decision context. The record makes local evidence, limitations, subgroup findings, uncertainty, and the resulting decision visible in one place.

It complements the broader model-selection and reproducibility material in **AIHE-B05**. It is surfaced independently under Chapter 11 because local validation is a distinct institutional responsibility rather than merely an appendix to model construction.

## Minimum record

A completed report should identify:

1. the intended and excluded uses, population, setting, decision window, and accountable owner;
2. the exact model, software, interface, threshold, and data period evaluated;
3. the local cohort, inclusion and exclusion criteria, missing data, reference standard, and provenance;
4. discrimination, calibration, threshold-specific performance, and clinically meaningful error consequences;
5. subgroup and intersectional findings, including uncertainty and sample-size limitations;
6. workflow fit, human–AI interaction, response capacity, overrides, delays, and balancing measures;
7. clinical utility or decision usefulness relative to the comparator;
8. unresolved evidence gaps and the evidence needed before greater dependence or scale; and
9. the decision: proceed, proceed with conditions, silent evaluation, limited pilot, recalibrate, restrict, defer, replace, or stop.

## Responsible use

This resource is a decision-support and notes aid. It does not establish regulatory compliance, clinical effectiveness, economic value, or legal acceptability. Replace synthetic examples with governed local evidence, preserve the evidence cut-off and system version, and apply the clinical, privacy, cybersecurity, quality, regulatory, and institutional requirements relevant to the intended use.

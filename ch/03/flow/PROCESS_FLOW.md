# Chapter 3: Human–AI Decision Systems and Managerial Action

Chapter-level decision process aligned to the fifteen-chapter reference edition.

## Process steps

### 1. Define the decision and user
Specify the output, user, decision window, consequence, and available action.

### 2. Set thresholds and abstention zones
Choose action, review, and deferral thresholds in relation to uncertainty and response capacity.

### 3. Design meaningful human oversight
Define review duties, context, authority, override, escalation, documentation, and fallback.

### 4. Embed the output in a signal-to-action pathway
Connect dashboard or model output to feasible action and accountable ownership.

### 5. Measure human–AI and managerial performance
Assess uptake, overrides, delay, decision quality, outcomes, workload, and balancing harms.

### 6. Feed results into learning and governance
Adjust thresholds, workflow, training, communication, or use restrictions.

## Illustrative application

A deterioration alert flags more patients than the rapid-response team can review, requiring threshold, abstention, and capacity redesign.

# AIHE-E01: Circular-Economy and Sustainable-AI Assessment

Core consolidated process aligned to Chapter 13 and Resource Set E.

## Process steps

### 1. Define the service output and lifecycle boundary
Clarify the functional unit, service requirement, assets, resources, and constraints relevant to Circular-Economy and Sustainable-AI Assessment.

### 2. Apply the circularity hierarchy
Evaluate avoidance, reduction, sharing, maintenance, repair, reuse, refurbishment, recovery, and disposal.

### 3. Measure economic and environmental effects
Document lifecycle cost, energy, water, carbon, materials, residual value, resilience, equity, and rebound.

### 4. Compare options under uncertainty
Use common boundaries, safety requirements, sensitivity, and non-compensatory safeguards.

### 5. Decide, contract, and monitor
Set procurement, asset-life, take-back, disposition, annual review, and corrective-action requirements.

## Illustrative application

Apply the healthcare circularity hierarchy while preserving safety, quality, resilience, equity, and accountability.

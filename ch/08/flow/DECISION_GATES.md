# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Translate the decision into a model specification** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Select the analytic or simulation method** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Assemble data, parameters, and evidence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Implement and verify** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Validate for the intended use** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Characterize uncertainty and value of information** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Communicate and archive** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Define the service problem and procurement specification** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Scan the market and challenge claims** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Run a workflow-based demonstration** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Conduct multidisciplinary due diligence** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Evaluate pricing, risk sharing, and total cost** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Negotiate enforceable lifecycle terms** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 7: Approve conditionally and monitor** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

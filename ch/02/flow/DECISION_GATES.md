# Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Confirm strategic and service requirements** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 2: Inventory infrastructure and data assets** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 3: Assess interoperability and data fitness** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 4: Assess resilience, security, and technical debt** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 5: Identify readiness gaps and owners** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.
- **Gate 6: Authorize readiness or restrict ambition** — confirm evidence, accountable owner, unresolved uncertainty, and authority before advancing.

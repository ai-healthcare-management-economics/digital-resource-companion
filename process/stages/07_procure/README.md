# Stage 7: Procure and contract

Translate the defined service need into vendor evidence requirements, due diligence, acceptance testing, pricing, audit rights, update control, continuity, portability, remedies, and exit.

**Primary chapters:** 9

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B04; AIHE-C03

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D01

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE09; AIHE-D; AIHE-EX08; AIHE-EX09

**Typical output:** Procurement specification, vendor comparison, contractual requirements, price and volume conditions, acceptance tests, and exit position.

**Decision gate:** Can the institution enforce the evidence, monitoring, change, continuity, and exit conditions required for safe use?

This stage is part of the Master Institutional Decision Lifecycle and should be adapted to the specific jurisdiction, institution, population, system version, evidence cut-off, and accountable authority.

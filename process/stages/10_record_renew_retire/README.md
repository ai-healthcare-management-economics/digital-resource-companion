# Stage 10: Assemble the decision record and renew or retire

Integrate evidence, economics, governance, implementation, monitoring, conditions, dissent, and accountability into a versioned decision record and make an explicit continue, redesign, restrict, replace, or retire decision.

**Primary chapters:** 12

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D05; AIHE-P; AIHE-AE

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-B02; AIHE-PY29; AIHE-PY40; AIHE-CASE12

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-C03; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D05

**Typical output:** Versioned AI-HED or equivalent decision dossier, final recommendation, review date, renewal or de-adoption decision, and transition record.

**Decision gate:** Is continuing use still preferable to redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement under current evidence and alternatives?

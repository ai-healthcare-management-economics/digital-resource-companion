# Stage 10: Assemble the decision record and renew or retire

Integrate evidence, economics, governance, implementation, monitoring, circularity, conditions, dissent, and accountability into a versioned decision record and make an explicit continue, redesign, restrict, replace, or retire decision.

**Primary chapters:** 12; 14; 15

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-C03; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D05

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D05; AIHE-P; AIHE-AE

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-E03; AIHE-E04; AIHE-E05; AIHE-PY29; AIHE-CASE12

**Typical output:** Versioned AI-HED or equivalent decision dossier, final recommendation, review date, renewal or de-adoption decision, and transition record.

**Decision gate:** Is continuing use still preferable to redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement under current evidence and alternatives?

This stage is part of the Master Institutional Decision Lifecycle and should be adapted to the specific jurisdiction, institution, population, system version, evidence cut-off, and accountable authority.

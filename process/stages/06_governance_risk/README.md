# Stage 6: Assess governance and institutional risk

Evaluate accountability, human oversight, ethics, equity, privacy, cybersecurity, resilience, trust, contestability, communication, and institutional risk as connected requirements.

**Primary chapters:** 10

**Primary resources:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-C04; AIHE-C05; AIHE-RG04

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE10; AIHE-AB; AIHE-AC; AIHE-N

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02; AIHE-A04

**Typical output:** Integrated institutional-risk review, accountability matrix, transparency record, safeguards, and escalation authority.

**Decision gate:** Are mandatory safeguards satisfied and are residual risks explicitly owned and governable?

# Process Dependency Map

This map identifies recommended preceding records and the principal records typically used at each stage. It is a navigation aid, not a rule that every project must complete every resource. Local law, governance, risk, evidence maturity, and decision consequence may require a different sequence or additional records.

## Stage 1: Define the decision

**Recommended preceding records:** None — this stage initiates the decision.
**Primary records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02
**Supporting records:** AIHE-RG07; AIHE-CASE01
**Typical output:** Approved decision and use-case specification with decision rights and an evidence plan.
**Decision gate:** Is the problem sufficiently important and specified to justify further assessment?

## Stage 2: Assess readiness and data

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02
**Primary records:** AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE02; AIHE-A; AIHE-U
**Typical output:** Readiness profile, remediation plan, data-provenance record, and decision on whether to strengthen foundations or continue.
**Decision gate:** Are critical prerequisites adequate for the intended level of consequence and dependence?

## Stage 3: Establish evidence and quality requirements

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04
**Primary records:** AIHE-C01; AIHE-C02; AIHE-EX05; AIHE-RG02; AIHE-RG06
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE05; AIHE-CASE11; AIHE-V
**Typical output:** Evidence plan, local-validation specification, monitoring indicators, acceptance thresholds, and corrective-action route.
**Decision gate:** Is the planned evidence proportionate to intended use, uncertainty, and consequence?

## Stage 4: Model cost, value, affordability, and HTA

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04
**Primary records:** AIHE-B01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B03; AIHE-B04
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE06; AIHE-CASE07; AIHE-B; AIHE-M
**Typical output:** Economic specification, lifecycle cost model, benefit-realization register, affordability profile, and conditional economic recommendation.
**Decision gate:** Does the intervention have a defensible value proposition and a financeable path under realistic scenarios?

## Stage 5: Select the decision model and characterize uncertainty

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B04
**Primary records:** AIHE-B05; AIHE-RG05
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE08; AIHE-EX04; AIHE-EX06; AIHE-EX15; AIHE-EX16
**Typical output:** Model specification, reproducibility record, uncertainty analysis, research priorities, and decision thresholds.
**Decision gate:** Is model complexity justified, credible, reproducible, and decision-relevant?

## Stage 6: Assess governance and institutional risk

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02; AIHE-A04
**Primary records:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-C04; AIHE-C05; AIHE-RG04
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE10; AIHE-AB; AIHE-AC; AIHE-N
**Typical output:** Integrated institutional-risk review, accountability matrix, transparency record, safeguards, and escalation authority.
**Decision gate:** Are mandatory safeguards satisfied and are residual risks explicitly owned and governable?

## Stage 7: Procure and contract

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B04; AIHE-C03
**Primary records:** AIHE-D01
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE09; AIHE-D; AIHE-EX08; AIHE-EX09
**Typical output:** Procurement specification, vendor comparison, contractual requirements, price and volume conditions, acceptance tests, and exit position.
**Decision gate:** Can the institution enforce the evidence, monitoring, change, continuity, and exit conditions required for safe use?

## Stage 8: Implement, monitor, and control change

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D01; AIHE-D02; AIHE-C01
**Primary records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D03; AIHE-D04; AIHE-D05; AIHE-RG03
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE04; AIHE-CASE12; AIHE-EX10; AIHE-EX11; AIHE-EX12
**Typical output:** Staged implementation record, monitoring cadence, incident record, change-control record, benefit evidence, and updated decision dossier.
**Decision gate:** Does real-world use remain within approved performance, safety, workload, equity, cost, and governance conditions?

## Stage 9: Apply circular and sustainable-value analysis

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D01
**Primary records:** AIHE-E01; AIHE-E02; AIHE-E03; AIHE-E04; AIHE-E05
**Supporting records:** AIHE-CASE13; AIHE-CASE14; AIHE-CASE15; AIHE-CE1; AIHE-CE2; AIHE-CE6
**Typical output:** Circularity assessment, asset-option appraisal, circularity-adjusted evaluation, disposition plan, and sustainable-value dashboard.
**Decision gate:** Does the preferred option preserve health-system value while controlling material, environmental, resilience, and disposition burdens?

## Stage 10: Assemble the decision record and renew or retire

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-C03; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D05
**Primary records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D05; AIHE-P; AIHE-AE
**Supporting records:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-E03; AIHE-E04; AIHE-E05; AIHE-PY29; AIHE-CASE12
**Typical output:** Versioned AI-HED or equivalent decision dossier, final recommendation, review date, renewal or de-adoption decision, and transition record.
**Decision gate:** Is continuing use still preferable to redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement under current evidence and alternatives?

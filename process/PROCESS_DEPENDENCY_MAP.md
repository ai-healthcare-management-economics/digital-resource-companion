# Decision-Process Dependencies

This navigation aid links the four-part, twelve-chapter book to an iterative institutional decision process. It is not a mandatory sequence or an authorization instrument. Decision states are distinct from implementation stages.

## Stage 1: Define the decision

Specify the service problem, population, complete intervention, realistic comparators, outcomes, value mechanism, stakeholders, and accountable authority.

**Primary chapters:** 1

**Primary resources:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-RG07; AIHE-CASE01

**Recommended preceding records:** None — this stage initiates the decision.

**Typical output:** Approved decision and use-case specification with decision rights and an evidence plan.

**Decision gate:** Is the problem sufficiently important and specified to justify further assessment?

## Stage 2: Assess readiness and data

Evaluate institutional capability, data fitness, provenance, interoperability, lawful access, resilience, technical debt, workflow capacity, and prerequisite investment.

**Primary chapters:** 2

**Primary resources:** AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE02; AIHE-A; AIHE-U

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02

**Typical output:** Readiness profile, remediation plan, data-provenance record, and decision on whether to strengthen foundations or continue.

**Decision gate:** Are critical prerequisites adequate for the intended level of consequence and dependence?

## Stage 3: Establish evidence and quality requirements

Define validation, reporting, appraisal, quality-control, accreditation, patient-safety, and independent-assurance requirements before consequential use.

**Primary chapters:** 5, 11

**Primary resources:** AIHE-C01; AIHE-C02; AIHE-EX05; AIHE-RG02; AIHE-RG06

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE05; AIHE-CASE11; AIHE-V

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04

**Typical output:** Evidence plan, local-validation specification, monitoring indicators, acceptance thresholds, and corrective-action route.

**Decision gate:** Is the planned evidence proportionate to intended use, uncertainty, and consequence?

## Stage 4: Model cost, value, affordability, and HTA

Estimate lifecycle cost, pricing exposure, benefit realization, cost-effectiveness, budget impact, affordability, distribution, reimbursement, and HTA implications.

**Primary chapters:** 6, 7

**Primary resources:** AIHE-B01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B03; AIHE-B04

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE06; AIHE-CASE07; AIHE-B; AIHE-M

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A03; AIHE-A04

**Typical output:** Economic specification, lifecycle cost model, benefit-realization register, affordability profile, and conditional economic recommendation.

**Decision gate:** Does the intervention have a defensible value proposition and a financeable path under realistic scenarios?

## Stage 5: Select the decision model and characterize uncertainty

Choose a decision or simulation structure that matches the causal and operational problem; document assumptions, verification, sensitivity, scenarios, thresholds, and value of information.

**Primary chapters:** 8

**Primary resources:** AIHE-B05; AIHE-RG05

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE08; AIHE-EX04; AIHE-EX06; AIHE-EX15; AIHE-EX16

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B04

**Typical output:** Model specification, reproducibility record, uncertainty analysis, research priorities, and decision thresholds.

**Decision gate:** Is model complexity justified, credible, reproducible, and decision-relevant?

## Stage 6: Assess governance and institutional risk

Evaluate accountability, human oversight, ethics, equity, privacy, cybersecurity, resilience, trust, contestability, communication, and institutional risk as connected requirements.

**Primary chapters:** 10

**Primary resources:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-C04; AIHE-C05; AIHE-RG04

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE10; AIHE-AB; AIHE-AC; AIHE-N

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-A02; AIHE-A04

**Typical output:** Integrated institutional-risk review, accountability matrix, transparency record, safeguards, and escalation authority.

**Decision gate:** Are mandatory safeguards satisfied and are residual risks explicitly owned and governable?

## Stage 7: Procure and contract

Translate the defined service need into vendor evidence requirements, due diligence, acceptance testing, pricing, audit rights, update control, continuity, portability, remedies, and exit.

**Primary chapters:** 9

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D01

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE09; AIHE-D; AIHE-EX08; AIHE-EX09

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B02; AIHE-B04; AIHE-C03

**Typical output:** Procurement specification, vendor comparison, contractual requirements, price and volume conditions, acceptance tests, and exit position.

**Decision gate:** Can the institution enforce the evidence, monitoring, change, continuity, and exit conditions required for safe use?

## Stage 8: Implement, monitor, and control change

Use staged implementation, role and competency design, post-deployment monitoring, algorithmovigilance, incident reconstruction, corrective action, material-change review, and renewal controls.

**Primary chapters:** 4, 12

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D03; AIHE-D04; AIHE-D05; AIHE-RG03

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE04; AIHE-CASE12; AIHE-EX10; AIHE-EX11; AIHE-EX12

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D01; AIHE-D02; AIHE-C01

**Typical output:** Staged implementation record, monitoring cadence, incident record, change-control record, benefit evidence, and updated decision dossier.

**Decision gate:** Does real-world use remain within approved performance, safety, workload, equity, cost, and governance conditions?

## Stage 9: Reassess continuing value, affordability, and support

Reassess the current comparator, realized benefits, lifecycle costs, budget exposure, supplier support, infrastructure dependencies, continuity, residual value, and credible replacement or exit options.

**Primary chapters:** 6, 9, 12

**Primary resources:** AIHE-B02; AIHE-B03; AIHE-B04; AIHE-D01; AIHE-D05

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-CASE06; AIHE-CASE06B; AIHE-CASE09B; AIHE-CASE12; AIHE-PY10; AIHE-PY11; AIHE-PY17

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-A01; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D01; AIHE-D03

**Typical output:** Updated financial case, supportability and continuity assessment, current alternatives, and a justified renewal, renegotiation, restriction, replacement, or retirement recommendation.

**Decision gate:** Does continuing use remain justified, affordable, supportable, and preferable to current alternatives without compromising mandatory safeguards?

## Stage 10: Assemble the decision record and renew or retire

Integrate evidence, economics, governance, implementation, monitoring, conditions, dissent, and accountability into a versioned decision record and make an explicit continue, redesign, restrict, replace, or retire decision.

**Primary chapters:** 12

**Primary resources:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-D05; AIHE-P; AIHE-AE

**Supporting resources:** AIHE-C03; AIHE-B02; AIHE-PY29; AIHE-PY40; AIHE-CASE12

**Recommended preceding records:** AIHE-D02; AIHE-C03; AIHE-B01; AIHE-D05

**Typical output:** Versioned AI-HED or equivalent decision dossier, final recommendation, review date, renewal or de-adoption decision, and transition record.

**Decision gate:** Is continuing use still preferable to redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement under current evidence and alternatives?

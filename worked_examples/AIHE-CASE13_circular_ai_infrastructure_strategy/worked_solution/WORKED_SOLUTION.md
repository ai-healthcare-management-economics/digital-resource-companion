# Worked solution: Circular AI Infrastructure Strategy

## Interpretation

The circularity hierarchy directs the system to avoid or reduce new demand before purchasing additional hardware. Model optimization and scheduling have the lowest cost and material demand. Shared regional capacity offers high utilization and resilience but requires robust governance. The recommended strategy is to optimize first, use shared capacity for incremental demand, and defer a new cluster until need is demonstrated.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

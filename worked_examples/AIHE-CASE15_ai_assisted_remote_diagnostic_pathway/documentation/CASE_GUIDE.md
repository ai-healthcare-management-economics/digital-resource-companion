# AIHE-CASE15 - AI-Assisted Remote Diagnostic Pathway: Circularity-Adjusted Economic Evaluation

**Companion release:** V1.0  
**Primary alignment:** Chapter 15, *Circularity-Adjusted Economic Evaluation and Sustainable Value*  
**Release date:** 2026-09-02

## Decision problem

Compare functionally equivalent remote-diagnostic pathways using common economic and environmental boundaries, average and marginal consequences, resilience, residual value, rebound, and burden transfer.

## Analytical focus

- Patient travel and access
- Local clinical and technical resources
- Central specialist capacity
- Computing, network, energy, water, and equipment
- Diagnostic delay and downstream tests
- Average and marginal resource consequences
- Supported service life and realizable residual value
- Resilience and continuity
- Rebound and induced demand
- Spatial and intertemporal burden transfer
- Equity of effective access
- Double-counting tests

## Use

1. Define the current service and realistic alternatives.
2. Replace all synthetic assumptions with authorized local evidence.
3. complete the linked institutional records and workbook.
4. Record uncertainty, safeguards, accountable ownership, decision state, and next review.

All numerical examples and demonstration data are synthetic unless a documented source states otherwise. This case does not establish the suitability of any actual technology or service.

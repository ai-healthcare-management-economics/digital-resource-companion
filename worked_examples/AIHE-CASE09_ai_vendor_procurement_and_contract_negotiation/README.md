# AIHE-CASE09 — AI Vendor Procurement and Contract Negotiation

**Version:** V1.0 
**Primary chapter:** 9. Problem-First Procurement and Vendor Governance 
**Related companion resources:** AIHE-D01, AIHE-D, AIHE-EX08, AIHE-EX09, AIHE-EX17 
**Related Python applications:** AIHE-PY17, AIHE-PY25 
**Data classification:** Entirely fictional and synthetic

## Decision question

How should the hospital compare vendors when a lower price is accompanied by weak audit, security, update, and exit provisions?

## Analytical and application objectives

- Separate weighted comparison from mandatory safeguards.
- Translate evidence and monitoring requirements into contract terms.
- Compare price with total lifecycle exposure.
- Preserve portability, rollback, and exit rights.

## How to use the case

1. Read `scenario/SCENARIO.md` before opening the worked solution.
2. Identify the decision-maker, realistic comparators, evidence, costs, constraints, safeguards, uncertainty, and decision rule.
3. Use the wb in `workbook/xlsx/` or its LibreOffice Calc equivalent in `workbook/ods/`.
4. Draft a recommendation that includes conditions, monitoring, review dates, and an exit or renewal pathway.
5. Compare the reasoning with `solution/WORKED_SOLUTION.md`.
6. Transfer approved conclusions into governed copies of the related companion resources.

## Folder contents

- `scenario/` — the decision context and questions
- `data/` — synthetic inputs in CSV and JSON
- `workbook/xlsx/` — worked and blank Excel sheets
- `workbook/ods/` — equivalent LibreOffice Calc sheets
- `solution/` — one defensible interpretation, not the only acceptable answer
- `documentation/` — related-resource mapping and self-study and review guidance.

## Limitations

The case demonstrates structured reasoning. It does not constitute empirical evidence, professional advice, or a validated institutional decision instrument. Local use requires independent evidence review, multidisciplinary judgement, and notes of all adaptations.

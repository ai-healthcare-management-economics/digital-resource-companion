# Local Validation and Causal Evaluation of a Readmission Model

**Example ID:** AIHE-CASE11 
**Aligned chapter:** Chapter 11 - Evidence, Validation, and Real-World Evaluation 
**Status:** Synthetic demonstration only 
**Version:** V1.0 
**Release date:** 2026-08-07

## Decision question

Does local performance and observed service change support adoption, and what can reasonably be attributed to the AI-enabled pathway?

## Scenario

A fictional hospital validates a vendor readmission model and implements it with a new care-management pathway. Local discrimination and calibration are weaker than the vendor report, one language subgroup performs poorly, and a control hospital is used to distinguish programme effects from secular change.

## Objectives

- Assess local validity, calibration, and subgroup performance.
- Separate model performance from intervention effectiveness.
- Use a controlled before-after comparison.
- State limitations and continuing evidence conditions.

## Decision question

Which alert threshold produces an operationally safe balance between missed deterioration and review burden?

## Scenario

A fictional hospital evaluates an early-warning model for 250 patients per day. The rapid-response team can review no more than 35 alerts daily. Three thresholds have different sensitivity and specificity, so the team must evaluate the human-AI system rather than select the highest-sensitivity threshold in isolation.

## Objectives

- Translate sensitivity and specificity into daily workload.
- Connect threshold selection to response capacity.
- Quantify missed cases, false alerts, and unreviewed alerts.
- Use abstention or staged review when no threshold is adequate.

# Worked solution: Model Update, Incident Response, and Renewal Decision

## Interpretation

Version 2.4.0 breaches false-positive, alert-volume, response-time, and near-miss thresholds. Use should be restricted immediately, evidence preserved, and rollback to 2.3.1 performed where safe. Restart should depend on local testing of the model, interface, threshold, and workflow under realistic capacity. Renewal should require improved update notice, audit access, rollback support, and effectiveness evidence.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

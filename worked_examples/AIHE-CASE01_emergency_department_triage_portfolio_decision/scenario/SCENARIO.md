## Decision question

Which intervention should the hospital test first when AI, staffing, and process redesign compete for the same limited resources?

## Scenario

Northbridge Health System, a fictional regional provider, faces long emergency-department waits and inconsistent prioritization. The board must compare an AI triage system with additional triage nurses, a process-redesign programme, and a combined AI-plus-redesign option. The decision must account for lifecycle cost, expected clinical and operational benefit, implementation burden, reversibility, and equity risk.

## Objectives

- Specify realistic comparators rather than an undefined status quo.
- Make opportunity cost and implementation capacity visible.
- Separate technical capability from realized health-system value.
- Formulate a staged and reversible recommendation.

# Worked solution: Five-Year Lifecycle Cost, Pricing, ROI, and Benefit Realization

## Interpretation

Year one is strongly negative because integration and data work precede benefit realization. Later years improve, but the five-year result remains sensitive to volume-linked fees and the share of time converted into usable capacity. The organization should cap variable fees, price integration and monitoring explicitly, and link renewal to verified local benefits through a staged contract with a break clause.

## Decision record

A complete institutional record should document the comparator, assumptions, evidence cut-off, accountable owner, uncertainty, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, review date, and exit or renewal criteria.

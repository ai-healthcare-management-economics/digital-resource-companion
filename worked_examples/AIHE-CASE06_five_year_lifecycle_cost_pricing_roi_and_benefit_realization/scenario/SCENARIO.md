## Decision question

Does the proposed AI service create value after all lifecycle costs, pricing exposure, and benefit-conversion conditions are included?

## Scenario

A fictional health system evaluates an enterprise notes and workflow platform over five years. The proposal combines a base licence with volume-linked fees and requires substantial integration, training, monitoring, and update validation. Benefits ramp gradually and depend on adoption and whether released time is actually used.

## Objectives

- Build a complete lifecycle cost profile.
- Distinguish fixed, variable, internal, and exit costs.
- Model benefit ramp-up and pricing exposure.
- Calculate ROI, break-even, and downside conditions.

**Digital Resource Companion V1.0**

The Digital Resource Companion is the applied extension of the book. It provides editable institutional records, structured workbooks, worked examples, decision pathways, reference guides, process maps, and reproducible analytical resources.

The companion does not replace the book's reasoning. It supports application to a clearly specified jurisdiction, organization, population, workflow, intervention, comparator, technology version, evidence base, and accountable decision process.

## Choose the appropriate entry point

| Entry point | Recommended for | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Pages Web Companion** | Most users beginning online | Guided navigation by chapter, decision stage, form, workbook, worked example, and analytical method |
| **Reader and Practitioner Edition** | Readers, practitioners, managers, analysts, educators, and institutional teams | A complete accessible offline collection with forms, workbooks, cases, guides, selected analytics, catalogues, the printable companion, and the offline website |
| **Core Tools Pack** | Teams needing the principal institutional documents | The consolidated Core Resource Forms, consolidated Core Resource Workbook, practical guidance, licence, disclaimer, and manifest |
| **Canonical Source** | Researchers, developers, maintainers, advanced analysts, educators, and institutions | The complete authoritative editable, computational, machine-readable, process, publication, and website source collection |
| **Printable Digital Resource Companion** | Readers preferring a coherent readable reference | A book-like PDF and Word volume explaining the companion architecture, resources, cases, methods, and use |

Most readers should begin with the **Web Companion** or the **Reader and Practitioner Edition**. Use the **Core Tools Pack** when the immediate need is a compact set of institutional forms and workbooks. Use the **Canonical Source** when maintaining, extending, reproducing, auditing, or republishing resources.

## Recommended institutional sequence

1. Define the service problem, population, setting, intended use, accountable decision-maker, and realistic comparator.
2. Read the aligned book chapter and identify the smallest sufficient companion resource set.
3. Review the relevant worked example and distinguish its synthetic assumptions from evidence required locally.
4. Create a governed local copy of each selected record or workbook.
5. Replace illustrative content with authorized, versioned local evidence and preserve important unknowns or disagreements.
6. Complete multidisciplinary review proportionate to consequence, uncertainty, scale, reversibility, and institutional dependence.
7. Record the current decision state, conditions, mandatory safeguards, monitoring thresholds, transition triggers, accountable authority, next review date, and credible exit route.
8. Reconsider the decision after material change, new evidence, an incident, changed costs, renewal, restriction, replacement, or retirement.

## Version control and responsible use

Record the stable resource identifier, V1.0, date accessed, evidence cut-off, intervention version, accountable owner, review date, assumptions, and local modifications. Preserve the exact inputs, code, environment, and outputs used in consequential analysis.

The resources support education, structured analysis, research, institutional deliberation, and local adaptation. They are not validated clinical instruments and do not replace legal, regulatory, clinical, ethical, privacy, cybersecurity, procurement, accreditation, financial, or health technology assessment review.

Do not enter identifiable patient information, credentials, private keys, confidential contracts, or restricted institutional information into public browser demonstrations or uncontrolled files. A completed form, score, or computational result does not constitute institutional authorization, and no favourable aggregate result overrides a mandatory safeguard.

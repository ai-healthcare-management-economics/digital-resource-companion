Decision stage 1 · Chapters 1

Define the decision

Specify the service problem, population, complete intervention, realistic comparators, outcomes, value mechanism, stakeholders, and accountable authority.

Decision question: Is the problem sufficiently important and specified to justify further assessment?

Recommended preceding records

None — this stage initiates the decision.

Typical output

Approved decision and use-case specification with decision rights and an evidence plan.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

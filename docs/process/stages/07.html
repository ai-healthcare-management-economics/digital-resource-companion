Decision stage 7 · Chapters 9

Procure and contract

Translate the defined service need into vendor evidence requirements, due diligence, acceptance testing, pricing, audit rights, update control, continuity, portability, remedies, and exit.

Decision question: Can the institution enforce the evidence, monitoring, change, continuity, and exit conditions required for safe use?

Typical output

Procurement specification, vendor comparison, contractual requirements, price and volume conditions, acceptance tests, and exit position.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

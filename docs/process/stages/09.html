Decision stage 9 · Chapters 6, 9, 12

Reassess continuing value, affordability, and support

Reassess the current comparator, realized benefits, lifecycle costs, budget exposure, supplier support, infrastructure dependencies, continuity, residual value, and credible replacement or exit options.

Decision question: Does continuing use remain justified, affordable, supportable, and preferable to current alternatives without compromising mandatory safeguards?

Typical output

Updated financial case, supportability and continuity assessment, current alternatives, and a justified renewal, renegotiation, restriction, replacement, or retirement recommendation.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

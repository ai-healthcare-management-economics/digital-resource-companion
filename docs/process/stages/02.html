Decision stage 2 · Chapters 2

Assess readiness and data

Evaluate institutional capability, data fitness, provenance, interoperability, lawful access, resilience, technical debt, workflow capacity, and prerequisite investment.

Decision question: Are critical prerequisites adequate for the intended level of consequence and dependence?

Typical output

Readiness profile, remediation plan, data-provenance record, and decision on whether to strengthen foundations or continue.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

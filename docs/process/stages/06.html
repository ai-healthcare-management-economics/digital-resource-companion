Decision stage 6 · Chapters 10

Assess governance and institutional risk

Evaluate accountability, human oversight, ethics, equity, privacy, cybersecurity, resilience, trust, contestability, communication, and institutional risk as connected requirements.

Decision question: Are mandatory safeguards satisfied and are residual risks explicitly owned and governable?

Typical output

Integrated institutional-risk review, accountability matrix, transparency record, safeguards, and escalation authority.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

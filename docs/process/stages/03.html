Decision stage 3 · Chapters 5, 11

Establish evidence and quality requirements

Define validation, reporting, appraisal, quality-control, accreditation, patient-safety, and independent-assurance requirements before consequential use.

Decision question: Is the planned evidence proportionate to intended use, uncertainty, and consequence?

Typical output

Evidence plan, local-validation specification, monitoring indicators, acceptance thresholds, and corrective-action route.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

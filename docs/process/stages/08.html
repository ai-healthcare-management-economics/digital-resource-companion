Decision stage 8 · Chapters 4, 12

Implement, monitor, and control change

Use staged implementation, role and competency design, post-deployment monitoring, algorithmovigilance, incident reconstruction, corrective action, material-change review, and renewal controls.

Decision question: Does real-world use remain within approved performance, safety, workload, equity, cost, and governance conditions?

Typical output

Staged implementation record, monitoring cadence, incident record, change-control record, benefit evidence, and updated decision dossier.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

Decision stage 10 · Chapters 12

Assemble the decision record and renew or retire

Integrate evidence, economics, governance, implementation, monitoring, conditions, dissent, and accountability into a versioned decision record and make an explicit continue, redesign, restrict, replace, or retire decision.

Decision question: Is continuing use still preferable to redesign, restriction, replacement, or retirement under current evidence and alternatives?

Typical output

Versioned AI-HED or equivalent decision dossier, final recommendation, review date, renewal or de-adoption decision, and transition record.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

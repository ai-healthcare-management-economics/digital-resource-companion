Decision stage 4 · Chapters 6, 7

Model cost, value, affordability, and HTA

Estimate lifecycle cost, pricing exposure, benefit realization, cost-effectiveness, budget impact, affordability, distribution, reimbursement, and HTA implications.

Decision question: Does the intervention have a defensible value proposition and a financeable path under realistic scenarios?

Economic specification, lifecycle cost model, benefit-realization register, affordability profile, and conditional economic recommendation.

The pathway is iterative. Implementation activity does not establish permission: record the current institutional decision state, exact scope, conditions, authority, and review triggers separately.

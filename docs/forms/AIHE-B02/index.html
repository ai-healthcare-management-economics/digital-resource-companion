AIHE-B02 — Lifecycle Cost and Pricing Record

Record acquisition through exit costs, resource use, pricing exposure, opportunity cost, residual value, and evidence.

Keep economic resource cost, cash expenditure, and accounting expense separate. Where physical assets are material, record supported service life, maintenance, replacement timing, and realizable residual value net of recovery costs (book §§6.2.3–6.2.4).

Legacy alias(es): AIHE-M; AIHE-EX17

Browser drafting aid. Static cells preserve the current companion specification; blank cells are editable. This browser form is intended for orientation, scenario drafting, and local working copies.

AIHE-B03 — Benefit Realization and Capacity Conversion

Translate claimed benefits into measurable mechanisms, owners, baselines, conversion conditions, and realized results.

Browser drafting aid. Static cells preserve the current companion specification; blank cells are editable. This browser form is intended for orientation, scenario drafting, and local working copies. Do not enter identifiable patient information or confidential institutional data into a public browser page.

Use after drafting. Transfer the resulting content into the governed canonical record, preserve system and evidence versions, identify an accountable owner, and document review and exit conditions.

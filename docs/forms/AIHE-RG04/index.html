AIHE-RG04 — Regulatory, Standards, and Policy Update Register

Maintain date-stamped records for European, Romanian, and global legal, policy, standards, reimbursement, and guidance sources whose scope or implementation status may change.

Browser drafting aid. Static cells preserve the current companion specification; blank cells are editable. This browser form is intended for orientation, scenario drafting, and local working copies. It does not establish evidence sufficiency, safety, compliance, effectiveness, or approval. Do not enter identifiable patient information or confidential institutional data into a public browser page.

Use after drafting. Transfer the resulting content into the governed canonical record, preserve system and evidence versions, identify an accountable owner, and document review and exit conditions.

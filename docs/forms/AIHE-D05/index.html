AIHE-D05 — Material Change, Reauthorization, Renewal, Replacement, and Retirement Record

Assess material change, revalidation, current authorization, renewal, renegotiation, replacement, retirement, continuity, and the end of authorization.

Renewal is a new decision against current evidence and alternatives. A technical update or interface shutdown does not by itself constitute reauthorization or retirement. Document continuity, records, communication, support, contractual closure, and the end of authorization (book §§12.4–12.5).

Legacy alias(es): AIHE-EX11; AIHE-T; AIHE-J

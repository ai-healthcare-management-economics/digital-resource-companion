Guided decision pathways

Each pathway provides a small essential set, optional support, required inputs, expected output, and typical time. Add advanced resources only when the decision requires them.

Chapters 1–2 · 2–5 hours across one or more multidisciplinary sessions.

Define a new proposal

For: Executives, clinical leaders, project sponsors, and portfolio committees

Question: What service problem is being addressed, compared with what, and is the institution ready to proceed?

Essential resources

  • AIHE-A01 — Decision and Use-Case Specification
  • AIHE-A02 — Stakeholders, Decision Rights, and Participation
  • AIHE-A03 — Institutional Readiness Assessment
  • AIHE-A04 — Data Source, Provenance, and Fitness Record
Recommended supporting resources
  • AIHE-CASE01 — Emergency-Department Triage Portfolio Decision
  • AIHE-CASE02 — Readmission-Model Readiness and Data-Fitness Assessment
  • AIHE-RG07 — Common AI Adoption Failures and Corrective Actions

Inputs: Baseline evidence, proposed population and setting, realistic alternatives, known dependencies, and named decision owners.

Output: A problem-first proposal, comparator set, readiness position, evidence gaps, and a proceed, remediate, pilot, or stop decision.

Chapters 6–8 · One day to several weeks, depending on modelling complexity and evidence maturity.

Prepare an economic and affordability case

For: Health economists, finance teams, HTA specialists, service managers, and commissioners

Question: Do expected outcomes justify full lifecycle cost and budget exposure, and which uncertainties could change the decision?

Essential resources

  • AIHE-B01 — Economic Evaluation Specification
  • AIHE-B02 — Lifecycle Cost and Pricing Record
  • AIHE-B03 — Benefit Realization and Capacity Conversion
  • AIHE-B04 — Cost-Effectiveness, Budget Impact, and HTA Record
  • AIHE-B05 — Model Selection, Uncertainty, and Reproducibility
Recommended supporting resources
  • AIHE-CASE06 — Five-Year Lifecycle Cost, Pricing, ROI, and Benefit Realization
  • AIHE-CASE07 — Remote Monitoring: Cost-Effectiveness, Budget Impact, and HTA
  • AIHE-CASE08 — Imaging Prioritization Decision Model and Value of Information
  • AIHE-RG05 — Scenario Planning and Real-Options Worksheet

Inputs: Comparator, population, resource quantities and prices, outcome evidence, uptake, implementation assumptions, and uncertainty ranges.

Output: A transparent cost, affordability, value, uncertainty, and evidence-priority record.

Chapters 2, 5, 8, and 11 · Several days to several months, depending on consequence and study design.

Validate a system locally

For: Clinical, data, informatics, quality, patient-safety, and research teams

Question: Does the exact system and version perform acceptably in the intended local population, workflow, and decision window?

Essential resources

  • AIHE-A04 — Data Source, Provenance, and Fitness Record
  • AIHE-EX05 — Local Validation Report Template
  • AIHE-B05 — Model Selection, Uncertainty, and Reproducibility
  • AIHE-C01 — AI Quality-Control and Accreditation Evidence Plan
Recommended supporting resources
  • AIHE-CASE11 — Local Validation and Causal Evaluation of a Readmission Model
  • AIHE-RG02 — Reporting and Appraisal Tool Selection Guide
  • AIHE-RG06 — Diagnostic and Predictive Evidence Appraisal Checklist

Inputs: Versioned cohort and data period, intended use, thresholds, subgroup definitions, workflow, and response-capacity evidence.

Output: A local-validation report with limitations, subgroup findings, clinical utility, monitoring conditions, and decision recommendation.

Before completion

  1. Confirm the decision and realistic comparator.
  2. Choose the smallest sufficient record.
  3. Create a governed copy.
  4. Replace synthetic or generic content with local evidence.
  5. Record owners, assumptions, uncertainty, thresholds, review date, and exit conditions.

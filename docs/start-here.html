Responsible use

Resources are intended for education, structured analysis, decision support, research, and local adaptation. They are not validated clinical instruments and do not replace legal, regulatory, clinical, ethical, cybersecurity, procurement, accreditation, financial, or health technology assessment review.

Worked examples and demonstration datasets are synthetic unless explicitly stated otherwise. Record the resource version, evidence cut-off, intervention version, assumptions, and local modifications whenever a resource contributes to a consequential decision.

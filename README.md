The Digital Resource Companion is developed and maintained independently by Muthana Zouri and Carmen Marinela Cumpăt to accompany Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Management and Economics: Evidence, Value, Governance, and Lifecycle Decision-Making. The authors are responsible for its content, maintenance, and subsequent updates.


The **Digital Resource Companion** accompanies *Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Management and Economics: Evidence, Value, Governance, and Lifecycle Decision-Making*. It extends the book with practical resources for institutional decision-making, health-economic evaluation, implementation, governance, evidence assessment, lifecycle management, and lifecycle stewardship.

The companion follows the book's current **four-part, twelve-chapter structure**. It contains 19 core institutional records, 16 worked examples plus a common case-analysis method, 36 Python analytical modules, and current catalogues for the book's 29 principal figures and 38 tables (including two front-matter tables).

## Start here

Most users should begin with the **[GitHub Pages Web Companion](https://ai-healthcare-management-economics.github.io/digital-resource-companion/)** or the repository's [`START_HERE.html`](START_HERE.html).

For a downloadable collection, select the distribution that matches the intended use.

| Distribution | Recommended for | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Reader and Practitioner Edition** | Most readers, practitioners, managers, analysts, educators, and institutional teams | Guided pathways, core forms and workbooks, all worked examples, reference guides, analytical guidance, catalogues, the printable companion, and an offline Web Companion |
| **Core Tools Pack** | Users who need the principal institutional documents without the complete companion | Consolidated editable forms, a consolidated workbook, completion guidance, licence and limited-use information |
| **Canonical Source** | Researchers, advanced analysts, educators, developers, repository maintainers, and institutions requiring complete source material | The authoritative editable, computational, machine-readable, process, catalogue, publication, and Web Companion source collection |

The **Core Tools Pack is a convenience subset**, not a separate edition. It is included within both larger distributions and is also supplied as a standalone release asset.

## Repository structure

```text
chapters/          Chapter-aligned records, guides, analytical modules, and process resources
worked_examples/   Synthetic worked cases and the common case-analysis method
process/           Master lifecycle, guided decision stages, and dependency maps
bundles/           Consolidated forms, workbook, and Core Tools Pack
publications/      Printable and editable Digital Resource Companion
catalogs/          Resource, figure, table, case, module, and relationship catalogues
docs/              GitHub Pages Web Companion
.github/           Contribution, issue, and pull-request resources
```

The repository is the **Canonical Source**. Material under `docs/`, `publications/`, and `bundles/` is a synchronized distribution layer and should be regenerated from the canonical resources when source material changes. See [`SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`](SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md).

## Core resource families

The 19 core institutional records are organized into four families:

- **A — Decision framing, participation, readiness, and data**
- **B — Economic evaluation, lifecycle cost, benefits, affordability, and modelling**
- **C — Quality, risk, accountability, and communication**
- **D — Procurement, monitoring, material change, and lifecycle governance**

Use [`RESOURCE_INDEX.md`](RESOURCE_INDEX.md), the master catalogue, or the Web Companion to select the smallest sufficient set of resources for the decision.

## Worked examples and analytical resources

Worked examples use fictional scenarios and synthetic data unless explicitly stated otherwise. They support learning, methodological illustration, reproducibility, and local adaptation; they do not constitute empirical evidence for an institutional decision.

The Canonical Source contains 36 chapter-aligned Python modules, with Jupyter notebooks and supporting files where applicable. See [`PYTHON_ANALYTICS.md`](PYTHON_ANALYTICS.md).

## Responsible use

The resources support education, structured analysis, research, deliberation, and local adaptation. They are not validated clinical instruments and do not replace legal, regulatory, clinical, ethical, cybersecurity, procurement, accreditation, financial, accounting, or health technology assessment review.

Before institutional use:

1. define the jurisdiction, organization, population, workflow, intervention, comparator, and accountable decision-maker;
2. replace synthetic or illustrative inputs with authorized evidence;
3. record the relevant system and model version, evidence cut-off, assumptions, and local modifications;
4. preserve mandatory safeguards independently of aggregate scores;
5. define monitoring thresholds, review dates, material-change triggers, and an exit route; and
6. retain a controlled copy of the exact resource version used.

Do not enter identifiable patient information, credentials, private keys, confidential contracts, or restricted institutional information into public browser demonstrations or uncontrolled files.

See [`LIMITED_USE_AND_DISCLAIMER.md`](LIMITED_USE_AND_DISCLAIMER.md).
